## 2. Modern Web Guidance Architecture Decisions
- **Native `<dialog>` Modals**: Use native `<dialog>` + `showModal()` with `overscroll-behavior: contain` on `dialog:modal` and `dialog::backdrop` (`dialog` guide). Zero manual `overflow: hidden` body locks.
- **Responsive Drawer (`<= 900px`)**: Use native swipe/click accessible drawer with `inert` outside the drawer when collapsed on mobile (`navigation-drawer` guide) and automatic desktop pinned rail (`>= 901px`).
- **Compositor-Only Motion**: Animate only `opacity` and `transform`, honor `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` opt-in for non-essential motion, and provide a Pause toggle on any ambient runic pulse (`motion` guide).
- **Web App Installability (`manifest.webmanifest` + `sw.js`)**: Provide valid `id`, `start_url`, `display: "standalone"`, `192x192` + `512x512` PNG icons (exported from `mimir-head`!), and progressive `beforeinstallprompt` / `navigator.install()` hook (`install-web-app` guide).
- **CSP & DOM Safety**: Zero `innerHTML` / `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` for untrusted content; clean text nodes + sanitized DOM (`security` guide).
